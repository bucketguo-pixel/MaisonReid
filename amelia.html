Senior Fashion Designer

Madeleine

Quiet precision, graceful structure and a couture sensibility define every piece.

EducationFashion Institute of Technology
FocusWomenswear · Eveningwear · Couture

Elegant design shaped around the wearer

Madeleine graduated from the Fashion Institute of Technology in New York, with a focus on womenswear design, garment structure and couture eveningwear. Her academic training developed a deep understanding of proportion, three-dimensional tailoring and fabric application, while shaping a design language that feels both classic and modern.

As senior fashion designer at MAISON REID, she is responsible for luxury womenswear design, eveningwear development and private client couture projects. She transforms her design ideas into refined, expressive garments by carefully responding to each client’s body, temperament and occasion.

From initial sketches and fabric pairing to sample refinement and final garment construction, Madeleine maintains rigorous attention to every detail. In private couture work, she values deep communication with clients so that each piece not only answers a practical need, but also reflects a distinctive personal identity.

True elegance never needs to be loud; a refined cut and carefully judged detail are enough to reveal a woman’s unique presence.
