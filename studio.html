PRIVATE COUTURE STUDIO

MAISON REID is a private couture studio dedicated to refined design, precise craftsmanship and an elevated client experience.

Founded by Wendy Reid, the maison is built on a philosophy that unites Italian classic aesthetics, French elegance and a modern couture mindset. Each commission begins with conversation, listening carefully to the client’s personality, lifestyle, occasion and aesthetic preferences before any design direction is defined.

Working through consultation, concept development, fabric selection, precision tailoring and final refinement, the studio delivers garments that balance artistry, wearability and individuality. Every step is shaped by a commitment to quality, discretion and personal attention.

Supported by an experienced team of 18 professionals, MAISON REID continues to refine its internal systems, strengthen creative collaboration and pursue long-term brand development while maintaining the highest standards of service.
